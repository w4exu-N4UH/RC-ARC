Rowan ARS Field Day 2026 – Photos

Between Friday 6/26 and wrapping up on Sunday 6/28/26, under Mark K’s leadership, we had a fantastic field day. We made contacts, we grew friendships, and grew stronger in unity as a club. Needless to say, we’re looking forward to the 2027 Field Day event!

The Salisbury Post wrote a feature article and visited the event: Ham it up: Rowan radio society joins nat’l event by Janet Briggs
