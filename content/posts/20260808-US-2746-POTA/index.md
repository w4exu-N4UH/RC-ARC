On Saturday morning, August 8, 2026, Mark, William, Logan, Michael, Vik, Jim, Rick, Bob, Bryan, (Dick H. visited), and Darrell (Johnny couldn't be with us) all enjoyed a great POTA activation at the scenic overlook of [*Morrow Mountain State Park*](https://www.ncparks.gov/state-parks/morrow-mountain-state-park). The views from Morrow Mountain were splendid!

After we had a great lunch at [*Five Points Public House* (Instagram)](https://www.instagram.com/fivepointspublichouse/?hl=en) in Albemarle where waitress Becky and her colleagues served us some delicious food.

We're already eagerly looking forward to the next POTA activation.

* an HF station brought by Mark

## Antennas
For base stations, we used at least two end-fed half-wave antennas, several varieties of vertical antennas.

# POTA
We were at POTA park entity US-2746. Many of us had never done a POTA event before. We used FM phone, SSB phone and Mark did some CW SSB.
